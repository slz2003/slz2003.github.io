Hi there! I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Virginia, advised by Prof. Wenxi Wang. I received my undergraduate degree from Rice University in 2025, with a dual degree in Mathematics and Computer Science, supported by the Rice Trustee Distinguished Scholarship. During my undergraduate studies, I had the privilege of working with Prof. Xia Hu and Prof. Tegawendé F. Bissyandé.

My research focuses on trustworthy and controllable generative modeling across both autoregressive and diffusion paradigms. I study how to build generative models that can incorporate constraints, structured reasoning, and verifiable feedback as part of learning and generation, with the goal of improving reliability, controllability, correctness, and verifiability. More broadly, I am interested in principled generative systems that can reason about and satisfy complex specifications across domains, including code generation, theorem proving, language and visual generation.
